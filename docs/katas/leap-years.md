# Leap years

Decide whether a year is a leap year. Every fourth year is, except that every hundredth
year is not, except that every four hundredth year is.

Three nested exceptions is more branching than it looks, and it needs no strings, no
collections and no data structure of any kind — which is why it is the third step for
every language that has none of those.

## What it teaches

Arithmetic and branching, and the first kata whose test cases you have to choose rather
than copy: 1900 and 2000 differ, and a suite that omits one of them passes while the code
is wrong.

## Where it sits

Step 3 on the Constrained, Proof and effects, and Playfield tracks — `forth`, `cobol`,
`basic`, `lolcode`, `arm32`, `lean`, `ats`, `intercal`. It assumes FizzBuzz, and is the
only step 3 in the catalogue that asks for nothing a language might not have.

## Not available in

Nothing. It is the one kata here with no missing-primitive caveat at all.
